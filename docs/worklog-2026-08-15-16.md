## 3. Decisions taken, with reasons

**Shadow mode for identity matching.** The previous posture was circular:
per-cohort thresholds cannot be fitted without real scores, and real scores
cannot be collected without running the matcher, so "wait until validated"
forbade the only activity that could end the wait. Shadow runs the matcher and
records every score while being structurally unable to act — `judgeCalibration`
returns `observed`, mid-exam events carry `enforced: false`, and the server
checks its own level rather than trusting the client's outcome.

**Recognition weights not bundled.** A model's demographic error profile is a
property of its weights and is exactly what is unmeasured here. NIST FRVT
reports rates for named algorithms; an unattributed ONNX has none, so bundling
one would turn "we have not measured this" into "we cannot look it up".

**Avatars are initials, with no upload path.** The product matches faces against
a live capture *because* a photograph can be anyone's. A user-supplied face
image in a profile is one careless import from becoming an enrolment artifact.
No upload path means nothing to remember.

**Three departures from the style reference, measured not judged.** Its
"Verified" pill (white on mint) is 2.55:1 and its "Flagged" pill 4.37:1 — one
fails WCAG AA badly, the other misses. Reference hues kept verbatim where they
are signal; same-hue darker companions computed where text sits on them.

**The legal org name is not editable.** It is what a candidate saw on the
invitation they accepted; rewriting it would retroactively change who they
believed they were consenting to.

## 4. The research finding

`5dd1bc7` — RESULTS.md §10 listed this as the highest-leverage analysis
available without a new participant.

`capture.webcam_signals` fails its gate at `face_valid_fraction` **0.8162**
against 0.85. The open question was whether the shortfall was the participant
leaving frame, in which case excluding those windows would be legitimate.

**It is not.** Excluding absent windows moves 0.8162 → 0.8389; the gate still
fails, so the decision does not change the outcome and can stop blocking.

The finding underneath is that the metric measures the wrong quantity.
Presence was **97.3%** at 3s tolerance. Only 8.9% of windows are fully invalid;
**78.6% are partially valid** — and a partially valid window cannot be absence,
because somebody was there for it. Dropouts have a **median of 0.2 seconds**,
which is a blink. So `face_valid_fraction` measures per-frame **detector
reliability**, while the gate's wording implies **presence**. Different
quantities, different remedies.

Made repeatable in `analysis/face_validity.py` rather than left as a number in a
document.

4. Analysis layer must exclude pause spans from idle/thinking inference
   (§8.1 rule 4) — the events are emitted, nothing reads them.
5. 92 hardcoded hex literals remain, in data-visualisation surfaces where the
   colours carry meaning.
